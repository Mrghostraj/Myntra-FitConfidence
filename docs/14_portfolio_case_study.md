# Portfolio Case Study — Myntra FitConfidence

## Snapshot
**Role:** Product Management Case Study (independent concept)  
**Category:** Men's T-Shirts  
**Stage:** Discovery and planning; prototype implementation pending

## Challenge
Online T-Shirt shoppers may face uncertainty selecting sizes because size charts, brand/style variation, and product imagery do not always translate into a clear personal fit expectation.

## Discovery
I conducted an exploratory Google Forms survey. There were 16 submissions, with 14 recent online T-Shirt purchasers eligible for core analysis. In this sample:
- 78.6% named cross-brand sizing differences as their biggest challenge.
- 85.7% found personalized recommendations helpful.
- 64.3% found visual expected-fit representation helpful.
- 78.6% were comfortable sharing chest measurement.

These are self-reported directional signals from a small convenience sample, not market-wide estimates.

## Product direction
Proposed a transparent, product-measurement-based size recommendation with fit preference and explanation. Advanced scope includes optional purchase-history personalization and AI visual fit simulation.

## PM artifacts
- Problem statement and research methodology
- Survey findings and limitations
- PRD and user stories
- Impact vs Effort prioritization
- Product backlog and acceptance criteria
- User journey and Figma screen plan
- Technical feasibility and architecture proposal
- Metrics, experiment plan, risks, and roadmap

## Key product judgment
Prioritize a reliable explainable recommendation core before expensive CV/GenAI. AI image generation is not equivalent to fit accuracy; visuals should be labeled as simulations and evaluated.

## What I would validate next
- Conduct user interviews and usability tests.
- Build a sample product size dataset.
- Prototype the rule-based recommendation flow.
- Test recommendation comprehension and input burden.
- Evaluate fit satisfaction before claiming business impact.

## Outcomes
No production deployment, recommendation accuracy, conversion uplift, or return reduction has been measured yet. Update this case study with actual outcomes after implementation/testing.
