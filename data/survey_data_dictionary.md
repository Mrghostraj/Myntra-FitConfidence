## Data handling
- Summary uses n=14 respondents who answered Yes to recent online T-Shirt purchase.
- Avoid publishing timestamps, respondent identifiers, emails, or row-level raw responses.
- Percentages from multi-select questions use respondent count as denominator; options overlap.
